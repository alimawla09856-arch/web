Turning silver into green.

Argentum is a Lebanese company that recovers silver from used X-ray film. Green stands for both the environmental impact and the value we create.

Mission

To make silver recovery from medical waste the standard in Lebanon: safe for patient data, fair to our suppliers, and good for the environment.

Vision

A world where no X-ray film ends up in landfill, and every gram of silver returns to use.

Why Argentum?

Argentum is the Latin word for silver. It is also where silver’s chemical symbol, Ag, comes from.

Patient confidentiality

X-ray films carry patient names and medical information. We collect them in sealed containers and destroy them in a controlled process, and every hospital receives a certificate of destruction.

Environmental impact

Used X-ray film contains silver, a heavy metal that can pollute soil and water when film is dumped or burned. By collecting the film and recovering its silver, Argentum keeps it out of landfill, reduces the need for newly mined silver, and returns a valuable metal to the local economy. Every collection is weighed and documented, so our partners can report their own environmental contribution.
