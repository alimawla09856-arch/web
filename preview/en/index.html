Turning silver into green.

Your used X-ray film is worth money. We make it count.

Argentum buys, collects and securely destroys used X-ray film from hospitals and imaging centres across Lebanon, then recovers the silver inside. You get paid, patient data stays protected, and nothing goes to landfill.

01/04

Collection

We collect your used X-ray film on a scheduled date, in sealed containers. The film is weighed on site, and you receive a signed receipt and payment at the agreed rate.

  • Signed receipt
  • Payment at the agreed rate

02/04

Sorting & secure destruction

At our facility, the film is sorted and prepared in a controlled environment. Patient information is destroyed in the process, and you receive a certificate of destruction.

  • Certificate of destruction

03/04

Silver extraction & refining

Silver is recovered from the film through a safe, controlled process, then refined and tested for purity.

  • Purity test

04/04

Back in the local economy

Silver back to market

The recovered silver is sold to silver shops and refiners at the market price, with an assay report for every batch. The remaining materials are handled responsibly.

  • Assay report for every batch
