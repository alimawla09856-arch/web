Questions hospitals ask us

Do you pay for the film?

Yes. Your film is weighed on site and you are paid at the agreed rate, with a signed receipt.

How is patient data protected?

We collect film in sealed containers and destroy it in a controlled process. Every hospital receives a certificate of destruction.

Which areas do you cover?

We collect from hospitals, imaging centres and clinics across Lebanon, from Beirut to Akkar and the Bekaa.

How quickly do you respond?

We respond to every collection request within 48 hours to agree a date.

Do you take archived film?

Yes. We collect archived films and ongoing film waste. If you are not sure what you have, choose “Not sure” in the form and we will check.

Which documents do we receive?

A signed receipt with the weight at collection, and a certificate of destruction after processing. Any Argentum certificate can be verified on this website.
